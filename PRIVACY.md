# Privacy

The Owner Statement Agent is a set of instructions that runs inside your own Claude or ChatGPT account.

- Sokage AI receives nothing. The agent has no server, no tracking, no analytics and no network calls of its own.
- The statement or export you give it is handled by the AI provider you chose, under that provider's own privacy terms.
- The agent never sends anything to anyone. It drafts; you decide what to send.
